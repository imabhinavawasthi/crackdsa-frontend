### Core Intuition
The primary goal here is to optimize mathematical operations that would normally take O(N) or O(N^2) time down to O(log N) or O(sqrt N) using mathematical properties and number theory.

# Common Pitfalls
<Warning>
- **Integer Overflow:** Mathematical operations easily exceed standard 32-bit integer limits. Always use 64-bit integers (`long long` in C++, `long` in Java) for intermediate accumulations.
- **Modulo Properties:** Forgetting to apply modulo at every step of addition/multiplication, not just at the end.
</Warning>
